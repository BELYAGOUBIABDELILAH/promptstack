# How to make a Memdeklaro self-declaration of identity?

**Category:** General  
**Source:** prompts.chat

## Prompt

> How to make a Memdeklaro self-declaration of identity?
> 
> 1. Visit the Memdeklaro website at https://memdeklaro.org
> 2. Fill out the self declaration form and click Generate
> 3. Download the self declaration as a PDF or JPEG file
